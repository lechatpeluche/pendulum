About the model & numerical accuracy

Each rod is massless and rigid, with a point mass at its far end. Angles are absolute, not relative to the previous rod. Every chain uses the same spatial scale and simulation clock. Nearly identical pendulums can overlap exactly; no artificial separation is added.

The coupled mass-matrix equations are solved with Cholesky factorization and adaptive Dormand–Prince 5(4) integration. Local error tolerances are 10⁻¹¹ absolute and 10⁻⁹ scaled; steps are at most 1/120 s. The energy readout is a numerical diagnostic, not an energy correction.

Chaotic motion eventually diverges even with tight tolerances. Local error control cannot guarantee exact long-term trajectories or exact energy conservation. If computation cannot keep up, simulation time advances more slowly, with a compute-limit notice. Unfinished wall-clock demand is not replayed later. Hidden tabs freeze simulation time.

Trails show only the last 10 simulation seconds, sampled at 120 Hz. They are a rendering approximation, not extra physical forces; very fast turns may be undersampled.
